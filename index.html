Gold Standard DIY

The Corsi-Rosenthal (CR) Box Air Purifier

Designed by environmental engineers Dr. Richard Corsi & Jim Rosenthal. Uses 4 MERV-13 filters and a 20" box fan to create a massive surface-area filtration cube with negligible static resistance.

Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) 550 - 620 CFM
Estimated Build Cost ₹2,200 ($28)
Commercial Purifier Equiv. ₹18,000+ ($220)

How to Assemble in 25 Minutes:

  1. Arrange 4 MERV 13 filters in a square cube with printed airflow arrows pointing INWARD.
  2. Tape seams securely with duct tape to form an airtight filter tower.
  3. Place 20" box fan on top blowing UPWARD / OUTWARD into the room.
  4. Pro-Tip (Shroud): Tape a cardboard donut shroud over the front fan rim to prevent air recirculation vortices and boost CADR by 25%.
